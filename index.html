Math of Risk to Reward (1:3 R:R)

You do not need a high win rate to be profitable. With a 1:3 ratio:

  • Sample size: 10 Trades
  • Risk: $100 per trade
  • Reward: $300 per trade
  • Win Rate required: 30% (3 wins out of 10)

Losses: 7 x -$100 = -$700
Wins: 3 x +$300 = +$900
Net Profit: +$200

Core Trading Concepts


What is Sample Size?

A set of consecutive trades (e.g., 20–100 trades) evaluated under fixed rules without changing strategy mid-way. Single trades are random noise; only large samples prove an edge.


What is an Edge?

A statistical advantage over the market that produces a positive expected value (EV) across a large sample size of trades.


Impact of Execution:

Even the best edge fails if execution is poor (e.g., chasing trades, early exits, emotional entries). Perfect execution bridges strategy to profitability.
