Knowledge Base: Risk to Reward Ratio Formula

When analyzing a sample size of 50 trades, your win rate determines how aggressive your risk-to-reward (R:R) ratio needs to be to remain profitable. Assuming you risk a fixed amount of $100 per trade:

Risk-to-Reward Ratio 30 Wins / 20 Losses (60% Win Rate) 25 Wins / 25 Losses (50% Win Rate)
1:1 RR (Win: $100 / Loss: $100) +$1,000 Profit $0 (Break-Even)
1:2 RR (Win: $200 / Loss: $100) +$4,000 Profit +$2,500 Profit
1:3 RR (Win: $300 / Loss: $100) +$7,000 Profit +$5,000 Profit
