⚠️ Brokers & Execution Model

Market Maker (B-Book) vs. ECN/STP Broker:

  • Market Maker: Takes the opposing side of your trade. If you win, they lose. This introduces a structural conflict of interest, often leading to artificial slippage, wider spreads during volatility, or re-quotes.
  • ECN Broker (True Direct Market Access): Routes orders directly to liquidity providers (banks/institutions). They profit purely on a fixed commission or tight spread, aligning their interests with reliable execution.

Core Trading Concepts


What is Sample Size?

A set of consecutive trades (e.g., 20–100 trades) evaluated under fixed rules without changing strategy mid-way. Single trades are random noise; only large samples prove an edge.


What is an Edge?

A statistical advantage over the market that produces a positive expected value (EV) across a large sample size of trades.


Impact of Execution:

Even the best edge fails if execution is poor (e.g., chasing trades, early exits, emotional entries). Perfect execution bridges strategy to profitability.
